# Source document review

## Scope and themes

The private source is *Antibody/Biologics-Based Therapeutics in the Treatment of Autoimmune Disease* (24 August 2026; SHA-256 `FA2D0C08124BC056F32F216F653352B1EF6D6667B2717250B23BA3FD5A1695F3`). It develops three themes: translation of TNF neutralization from collagen-induced arthritis; diversification into B-cell, IL-17, FcRn, complement and CD40L interventions; and emerging precision or delivery platforms.

Named agents are infliximab, adalimumab, etanercept, rituximab, tocilizumab, obinutuzumab, ianalumab, telitacicept, bimekizumab, sonelokimab, efgartigimod, ravulizumab and dazodalibep. Named direct systems include TNF, CD20, BAFF-R, BAFF, APRIL, IL-17A/F, FcRn, C5 and CD40L. Diseases include rheumatoid arthritis, lupus nephritis/SLE, Sjögren disease, psoriatic arthritis and generalized myasthenia gravis.

## Studies and figures

Clinical studies cited include REGENCY, the ianalumab phase 2b study, telitacicept phase 3, BE OPTIMAL/BE COMPLETE, the sonelokimab phase 2 study, ADAPT, MycarinG, CHAMPION-MG, dazodalibep phase 2, R4RA and STRAP. Three scientific figures depict collagen-induced arthritis/TNF neutralization, B-cell-directed mechanisms, and sonelokimab architecture. See `source_image_inventory.md`.

## Claims requiring verification

- Approval must be resolved by indication, jurisdiction and date. Obinutuzumab is now FDA-approved with standard therapy for active lupus nephritis; ianalumab's FDA breakthrough designation is not approval.
- Telitacicept has China-specific approval for active SLE; this cannot be generalized globally.
- Dazodalibep remains investigational in the catalogued Sjögren indication.
- R4RA's inference differs according to histological versus molecular B-cell classification; STRAP did not validate the simple B-cell-rich/poor classifier.
- Source citation counts are dynamic and not used as evidence quality measures.
- Preclinical nanoparticle, microneedle and CAR-T reports cannot be represented as established antibody therapy.

## Computationally suitable material

Direct drug–target relations, indication-specific status, target–pathway membership, typed evidence networks, clinical-study coverage and modality distributions are auditable. Comparative response ranking and formal enrichment are not justified by this small, selected, heterogeneous set.

## Excluded assignment material

The public project excludes the cover sheet, module and student identifiers, word-count declaration, university branding, submission dates, AI declaration and assessment-only imagery. The original document remains unmodified in a Git-ignored directory.

## Scientific limitations

The source is a narrative coursework document rather than a systematic review; selection criteria and search methods are absent. It mixes animal, trial and regulatory evidence, occasionally relies on sponsor-linked or review citations, and predates or incompletely captures status changes. Its figures have unclear public-reuse rights. It therefore supplies context and candidate entities, not unquestioned authority.
