# Figure captions and sources

All figures are generated by `src.visualization` from the processed CSV tables. Figures 1–10 respectively show the workflow; mechanism counts; direct drug–target bipartite graph; observed-evidence heatmap; heterogeneous network; pathway membership; modality counts; study-phase coverage; R4RA/STRAP evidence; and indication-specific translational state. Grey in Figure 4 means “not documented in this curated scope,” not confirmed non-interaction. Source-level citations are stored in the underlying rows. Colors use the Okabe–Ito palette where applicable.
