# Precision immunotherapy evidence

R4RA enrolled 164 patients with RA and inadequate response to TNF inhibition and compared rituximab with tocilizumab after synovial biopsy. The histological B-cell classification did not produce a significant primary comparison. A molecular B-cell-low subgroup showed a stronger differential signal favoring tocilizumab, demonstrating that assay definition materially affects inference.

STRAP/STRAP-EU randomized 226 biologic-naive patients to rituximab, tocilizumab or etanercept after biopsy. In the B-cell-poor primary population, ACR20 response at week 16 did not differ between grouped etanercept/tocilizumab and rituximab (60% versus 59%; reported OR 1.02, p=0.97). Thus the simple rich/poor classifier was not validated as a treatment-selection test.

Subsequent RNA-sequencing analyses reported multigene response models and external validation against R4RA samples. These findings strengthen the research case for multidimensional tissue profiling, but retrospective model performance is not prospective clinical utility. Biopsy feasibility, assay standardization, sampling heterogeneity, treatment interactions, calibration and independent prospective validation remain barriers. No companion diagnostic is claimed here.
