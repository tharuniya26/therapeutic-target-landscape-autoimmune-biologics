# Research questions and measurable objectives

## Primary question

How can the molecular targets, immune pathways and clinical evidence of established and emerging biologic therapies for autoimmune diseases be systematically mapped and compared using reproducible computational methods?

## Secondary questions

The analysis asks which direct targets and pathway systems occur in the curated landscape; which agents share targets; how cytokine neutralization, receptor blockade, cell depletion, FcRn blockade and complement inhibition differ; how indication-specific development stages are distributed; and what R4RA and STRAP establish about tissue-guided therapy selection.

## Objectives

1. Curate at least the 13 source-named agents with an auditable indication, jurisdiction, verification date and primary evidence link.
2. Validate HGNC symbols and UniProt accessions for every direct protein target.
3. Encode drug–target, drug–disease and target–pathway relations as separate typed edges.
4. Compute descriptive graph metrics and explicitly avoid equating degree with efficacy or biological importance.
5. Summarize clinical-evidence coverage by phase, disease and modality without comparing incompatible endpoints.
6. reproduce every table, network and figure from version-controlled code and test the principal integrity rules.
