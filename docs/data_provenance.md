# Data provenance

## Sources actually used

- FDA prescribing information for obinutuzumab, bimekizumab and ravulizumab; FDA orphan approval record for efgartigimod.
- UniProt records for canonical human protein identifiers and HGNC cross-identifiers.
- PubMed/PMC and publisher primary reports for R4RA, STRAP, REGENCY, ianalumab, telitacicept, bimekizumab, sonelokimab, ADAPT, CHAMPION-MG and dazodalibep.
- ClinicalTrials.gov or published registry identifiers when present in the principal report.
- The private coursework source only for candidate discovery, theme review and source-figure inventory.

The regulatory snapshot date is 2026-10-18. URLs, identifiers and verification status are embedded in each row. No ChEMBL bulk download or statistical enrichment service was required. Raw third-party content is not redistributed.

## Curation rules

Records were accepted only when target, disease context and evidence source were explicit. Status is never propagated from one indication or jurisdiction to another. Downstream pathway membership is maintained separately from binding evidence. Missing evidence is retained as missing. This version is a focused landscape, not an exhaustive drug census.
