# Clinical evidence methodology

Studies were selected if they directly supported a catalogued mechanism or addressed the prespecified precision-treatment question. Principal peer-reviewed trial reports were preferred; regulatory labels resolve approval status. Extracted fields include registry ID, phase, design, randomized sample size, comparator, primary endpoint, controlled result summary and a major limitation.

The landscape is qualitative and descriptive. Trial populations, endpoints, follow-up, background therapy and diseases are heterogeneous, so response percentages are not pooled and drugs are not ranked. R4RA and STRAP are analyzed as biomarker-utility studies, with prespecified primary findings distinguished from exploratory molecular analyses. Meta-analysis was not attempted.
